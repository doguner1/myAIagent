# Swarm Intelligence & Trend Prediction (MiroFish Architecture)

## Overview
Based on `666ghj/MiroFish`, this skill provides multi-agent audience simulation to predict how viewers, algorithms, and communities will react to content, technical announcements, or architectural decisions before deployment.

## Simulation Pipeline
1. **Persona Decomposition:** Instantiate synthetic personas representing target segments:
   - *The Cynic/Skeptic:* Looks for clickbait, shallow claims, or factual errors.
   - *The Enthusiast/Early Adopter:* Craves concrete details, model names, and practical value.
   - *The Casual Scroller:* Decides within 2 seconds whether to stay or swipe away.
   - *The Instigator:* Seeks debate, controversy, or strong polarizing points.
2. **Stress-Test Scenarios:**
   - **Hook Retention Test:** Does sentence 1 stop the scroll?
   - **Controversy & Comment Engine:** Does the ending or pinned comment compel typing a reply?
   - **Educational Substance:** Did the viewer actually learn something concrete?

## Application in Active Projects
- **Pixlabss & Doğukan Channels:**
  - Before rendering video clips, simulate audience reception on title, hook, and thumbnail contrast.
  - Reject vague promises ("bu araçlar") in favor of concrete, controversial, or specific claims ("Cursor AI vs Claude 3.7").
  - Formulate high-yield discussion questions for the pinned comment that maximize algorithmic engagement.
